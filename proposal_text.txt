Objective:  
The objective of AlchEx is to design and develop a cashless digital marketplace exclusively 
for students, where goods and services are exchanged without the use of money. The 
platform is inspired by the alchemical principle of Equivalent Exchange, which states that 
humankind cannot gain anything without giving something in return. AlchEx aims to 
encourage collaboration, skill-sharing, and mutual support among students by enabling fair 
and transparent exchanges within a trusted academic environment  
 
Scope:  
AlchEx will function as an exchange ecosystem with the following core features::  
1.​ User Authentication and Access Control:​
Students will be able to register and log in using verified .edu email addresses only, 
ensuring that the platform remains exclusive to students. There will be two types of 
users in the system: Students and Admins. Secure authentication and account 
management will be implemented for all users. 
2.​ Student Marketplace (Requests and Offers):​
Students will be able to create posts describing a request for a service or good, along 
with what they are willing to offer in return. Examples include academic help, sharing 
course resources, or small everyday items such as coffee or snacks. Other students can 
respond to a post by accepting the listed offer or proposing an alternative exchange. 
Each student can submit only one offer per post, while a student may create multiple 
requests simultaneously. 
3.​ Post Lifecycle and Status Management:​
Each post will have a defined active time period, after which it will automatically expire 
and be removed from the active listings. Every post will display a clear status such as 
Active, Accepted/In Progress, Successful, or Expired. By default, only active posts will 
appear on the main feed, while other statuses will be accessible through filters and user 
history. 
4.​ Acceptance, Communication, and Transaction Completion:​
The original requester will have full control to accept or reject incoming offers. Once an 
offer is accepted, a private chat will be enabled between the two students to coordinate 
the exchange. After the exchange is completed, both users can mark the transaction as 
successful. During the in-progress stage, either participant may choose to cancel the 
transaction before completion, after which the post status will be updated accordingly. 
5.​ Ratings, Reputation, and Leaderboard:​
After a successful exchange, students will be able to rate each other based on their 
experience. These ratings will contribute to a reputation score visible on user profiles. A 
leaderboard will be included to highlight top contributors based on successful 
transactions and ratings, with filters such as Today, Last Week, and Last Month. 
6.​ User Profiles and Filtering:​
Each student will have a profile displaying a profile picture and basic academic 
information, including name, email, batch, program, section, university, and campus. 
Students may also add a short personal description and optional social media links. The 
platform will provide filters and sorting options based on university, campus, batch, 
program, and section to help users find relevant exchanges easily. 
7.​ Admin Panel and Moderation:​
Admins will have a dedicated dashboard with moderation capabilities. They will be able 
to monitor posts, manage users, resolve disputes, remove inappropriate listings, and 
ban users if necessary. Admins will also have the authority to contact users directly for 
warnings or clarification in case of policy violations. 
8.​ Reporting and Dispute Handling:​
Students will be able to report a transaction if a mishap occurs, such as failure to deliver 
the promised service or good. Reported cases will be visible to admins, who can review 
the dispute and take appropriate action. 
9.​ AI-Assisted Moderation (Optional):​
If time permits, AI-based tools may be integrated to assist with content moderation by 
detecting spam, inappropriate content, or suspicious behavior, helping admins maintain 
a safe and fair environment. 
Conclusion:​
AlchEx proposes a unique, non-monetary exchange system tailored specifically for students, 
promoting fairness, collaboration, and community engagement. By combining structured 
moderation, transparent transactions, and the philosophy of equivalent exchange, the platform 
aims to create a trustworthy and engaging student-driven marketplace within the academic 
ecosystem.
